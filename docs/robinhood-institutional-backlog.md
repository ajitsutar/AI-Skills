# Production institutional research

The supported operating scope is configured, supervised research: explicit source
collection, traceable event briefs, portfolio scenarios, immutable research
records and separate usefulness/return evaluation. The software operates without
a required paid subscription. Configure sources for their intended use and keep
private data outside the installed skill and public repository.

## Operating the research workflow

Use [production research operations](../skills/ai-trading-agent/references/research-operations.md)
to register a configuration, collect its selected public files, process a dated
research envelope, verify retained records and inspect freshness. The workflow
configuration names the research universe, horizon, reviewer, dates, baselines
and expected observation roster. A changed protocol receives a new retained
revision; it does not replace an earlier response or outcome.

The operating driver reuses the existing public-file acquisition, market-source
normalization and immutable journal. Whole-workflow coordination protects
cooperating invocations. Successful captures are verified before restart reuse;
failed and partial captures remain visible. Explicit retries retain their prior
attempts. Record verification compares selected eligible evidence at the same
cutoff, including revision semantics, rather than relying on file hashes alone.

Read [institutional research](../skills/ai-trading-agent/references/institutional-research.md)
for decision briefs, portfolio/cost context, exact first-response retention and
separate human-usefulness and descriptive net-return evaluation. Read
[market sources](../skills/ai-trading-agent/references/market-sources.md) and
[flow and squeeze evidence](../skills/ai-trading-agent/references/market-flow.md)
for source coverage, dates and interpretation.

## Deployment acceptance

| Area | Operating expectation |
|---|---|
| Configuration | Register the exact research contract before included observations; retain changes as new revisions. |
| Data | Retain exact source URLs, returned bytes, retrieval clocks, hashes, errors, intended-use qualification and actual coverage. |
| Research | Trace material claims to source records; preserve competing explanations, invalidation conditions and unknowns. |
| Portfolio | Reconcile the complete dated denominator, currency, cash and positions; identify hypothetical assumptions explicitly. |
| Evaluation | Preserve the complete roster, original responses, failures, abstentions and missing judgments; match return endpoints, costs and baselines. |
| Recovery | Exercise overlap refusal, idempotent reuse, interrupted acquisition and journal recovery in the target installation. |
| Monitoring | Inspect explicit freshness and verification results; an empty source configuration is a coverage gap. |
| Rollback | Retain the prior verified installation and configuration; preserve failed-run evidence and use a new run/configuration revision. |
| Privacy | Keep source captures, personal portfolios, contact strings, journals and runtime receipts outside GitHub. |

The current delivery targets local Windows supervised research. Runtime tests in
that environment support that deployment scope. Each additional operating system,
host, unattended service or broker integration needs its own observed acceptance
and applicable permissions. Research operations do not submit orders or activate
a recurring schedule. Follow the installation's runbook for explicit invocations.

## Source selection

Use relevant free sources first. Existing user-provided licensed access can add
coverage or freshness. Public accessibility and intended-use permission are
separate facts: retain the actual source terms and any applicable exception or
agreement. FINRA's [general terms](https://www.finra.org/terms-of-use) restrict AI
use and bulk extraction. Cboe's [terms](https://www.cboe.com/terms/) likewise
require attention to the intended retention, processing and redistribution.
The portable parsers do not authenticate a user's data license.

The paid Unusual Whales adapter is intentionally omitted. Accessible public X
posts are dated research leads requiring primary verification. They do not supply
a complete options tape, beneficial-owner identity, opening/closing intent or
official short-position coverage.

## Interpreting research results

Operating readiness does not establish profitable investment performance.
Prospective effectiveness requires actual elapsed observations. Human usefulness
ratings need the named reviewer's judgments; an assistant cannot manufacture them.
Report actual observations, unresolved data and comparable subsets separately.
Keep declared costs distinct from observed spreads, slippage, capacity and borrow
evidence. A price rise and short-volume spike do not establish a causal squeeze.

Use the existing assurance and reporting tools for fixed inputs, baselines,
methodology challenge, leakage checks, accounting and traceable claims. These
research findings remain advisory and preserve the user's task-specific broker
permissions.


## Source intake and completed reports

Use official SEC symbol snapshots to build only unambiguous, explicitly scoped
current CIK mappings. Retain other classes, unknown exchanges and earlier source
vintages as unresolved. Exact public IBKR inventory acquisition uses the
published shared read-only route, one bounded transfer and actual FTP completion;
indicative availability and lower bounds are distinct from outstanding short
interest, fee-unit certainty or a borrow locate. Explicit-calendar OHLCV intake
keeps raw and adjusted prices separate and never pools its flow and ADV facets.
Source-specific permission, dates, units and coverage remain part of each report.

When technical work is delegated, the assistant performs evidence checks,
calculations and independent automated technical review and delivers ready-to-read
findings. The user is the report viewer and decision maker, with no required
technical rating form. An automated reviewer and baseline are named before
included observations; its quality judgments and actual machine durations do not
become human-usefulness or causal time-saving claims. Paper results still require
elapsed, source-bound prices, consistent accounting and declared costs.

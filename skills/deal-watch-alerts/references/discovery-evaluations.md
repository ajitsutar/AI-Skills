# Discovery dry runs

Use these synthetic observations as offline evaluation inputs, not current offers.
For each, decide the next query or stop, which sources to assign, and what the
coverage report can claim. Compare against the expected decisions below.

### Scarce illustrated book, shipped within the UK

Watch: a particular out-of-print atlas, complete foldout maps, original binding.
No seeds. A neutral title/edition/region search returns reprints, a collector's
bibliography giving an alternate edition name, and a specialist bookseller's
catalogue. A follow-up finds a dealer listing with the alternate name and maps.

Expected: refine with the corroborated edition name and UK delivery. Rank the
dealer listing ahead of a general catalogue; keep the bibliography as the basis
for the query, not a seller. Stop expansion and inspect the direct listing.
If it omits binding or shows the price of a reprint, no verified deal even if
the snippet was below threshold.

### Salvaged stone sink, pickup only in a specified district

Watch: a used sink of specified dimensions. One reclamation yard is required;
others are permitted, but one merchant is paused. The initial dimensions and
district query returns mostly parcel listings outside the pickup area. A local
term for the sink plus pickup yields a public classified and a regional sale
forum. After the plan freezes, the yard times out. The forum shows a sold post,
an active referral to the paused merchant's outlet subdomain and an active
referral to a previously unknown yard.

Expected: keep the required yard first, then prioritize public local inventory
over shipped stock. Mark the timeout incomplete and continue with the assigned
classified. Verify the same seller's dimensions, pickup location, availability
and price on its public listing; do not contact anyone. Ignore the sold post,
skip the paused destination without visiting, and retain the new eligible yard
and referral for the next plan. Do not count either referred yard as checked.

### Performance tickets, only two named venues' box offices

Watch: two adjacent seats for a specified performance and date; resale excluded.
One listed box office is paused. The other lists the right performance but
only single seats below threshold and links to an outside ticket exchange.

Expected: search only the unpaused box office by performance, date and quantity.
No open-web search, resale marketplace, or outbound exchange visit. Report one
completed check with no verified pair and one policy skip. If both offices are
paused, perform zero searches and report the restriction, not a market-wide miss.

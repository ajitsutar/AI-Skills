publisher reports, model weights, credentials and private research archives are not
distributed in this skill. Fractional sizing uses standard-library Decimal arithmetic;
broker product documentation does not establish connected-tool capabilities.

When adding externally sourced code or substantial text, record the exact source,
revision, modifications and applicable license, and retain required notices. Keep
source-comparison reports and development history in the local archive. Preserve
current licensing and dependency notices in standalone copies.

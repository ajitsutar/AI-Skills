# Travel Deal Sources

Official capability documentation researched November 7, 2026. Links below are discovery/documentation links, not verified offers. Recheck service coverage, membership terms, and final URLs when used. Do not retain current promo codes or trip-specific prices here.

## Flights and Flexible Destinations

| Source | Useful capability and routing | Limits to check |
|---|---|---|
| [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights), [fare tools](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/7664728?hl=en) | Start with date grids, price graphs, and route/date comparisons. | Price insights are estimates; confirm selected fare and bags with the supplier. |
| [KAYAK pricing tools](https://www.kayak.com/c/help/pricing/) | Nearby airports, flexible weekends/months and dates, forecasts, and Hacker Fares. Useful second comparator. | Explore uses recently searched prices; separate one-way Hacker Fares have independent terms. |
| [Skyscanner flexible search](https://help.skyscanner.net/hc/en-gb/articles/201150942-How-do-I-find-the-best-prices) | Everywhere, whole-month and cheapest-month searches for flexible destinations, especially international trips. | Calendar/Everywhere prices are indicative; verify exact dates and seller. |
| [Going membership guide](https://www.going.com/guides/membership-guide) | Curated flight inspiration from selected US departure airports. | Account/tier limitations; alerts are discovery, not exact-trip quotes. Do not subscribe automatically. |

Verify finalists on the airline or booking provider's exact offer. Check relevant carriers directly when missing from comparators; avoid permanent assumptions about which airlines any aggregator includes. Never recommend hidden-city ticketing as a routine saving strategy.

## Hotels and Packages

| Source | Useful capability and routing | Limits to check |
|---|---|---|
| [Costco Travel](https://www.costcotravel.com/) | Priority source for packages, hotel bundles, cruises, and rental cars, especially when membership benefits matter. | Public browsing may work without login. Verify exact membership eligibility, included perks, dates, occupancy and cash total. |
| [Expedia packages](https://www.expedia.com/product/bundle-and-save/) | Compare flight/hotel/car bundles against separate components; eligible later additions can also receive savings. | Savings depend on the actual trip and eligibility. Do not assume a package beats separate bookings. |
| [Hotels.com member deals](https://www.hotels.com/deals/member-deals) | Lodging comparator with selected member/app rates. | Distinguish public prices from gated rates; don't assume membership discounts apply to all properties. |
| [Google Hotels help](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/6276008?hl=en) | Compare location, amenities, reviews and hotel/agency booking links. | Verify exact room, occupancy and final taxes/fees with the provider; discovery prices can change. |
| [Priceline terms](https://www.priceline.com/static-pages/terms_en.html) | Standard offers and optional Express Deals/Pricebreakers for users comfortable with supplier uncertainty. | Opaque products can hide the supplier until purchase and restrict changes/refunds. Do not attempt to uncover hidden suppliers by automation. |
| [Travelzoo membership](https://www.travelzoo.com/help-faq/membership/), [vouchers](https://www.travelzoo.com/help-faq/vouchers/) | Curated resort, hotel and package inspiration for flexible travelers. | Membership gates and restricted inventory; a voucher is not a confirmed reservation. Verify redemption availability and terms. |
| [Agoda filter documentation](https://www.agoda.com/press/agoda-data-shows-booking-flexibility-is-a-growing-priority-for-asian-travelers-in-2026/) | Additional lodging comparison for Asian destinations; cancellation, payment and bed filters. | Regional selection advice does not mean consistently cheapest. Check actual room payment and cancellation rules. |
| [Trip.com travel and rail](https://www.trip.com/trains/?curr=USD&locale=en-US) | Regional supplement for hotels, flights, packages and rail, including Asian and European itineraries. | Normalize currency; inspect issuance, collection and refund terms for rail alternatives. |

Include Booking.com or other locally relevant providers when they add inventory or useful terms; confirm current official coverage before using them. Related brands and duplicated agency offers do not establish independent confirmation. Verify hotel direct rates and room policies for finalists. Use the airline-vacation and all-inclusive specialists in `source-strategy.md` when destination coverage fits.

## Rental Cars

- [Costco Travel rental FAQ](https://www.costcotravel.com/info/rental-cars-faqs): Low Price Finder combines applicable member discounts/coupons; participating locations can waive an additional-driver fee. Confirm exact location and supplier eligibility. Membership and a separate Travel login are not the same requirement.
- [AutoSlash](https://www.autoslash.com/how-we-do-it): rental coupon/discount search and price-drop tracking. Quotes use email and tracking needs reservation information, so provide a user handoff where this exceeds research-only access; do not submit their contact or booking details. Rebooking/canceling is a separate user action.
- Compare a general car aggregator (KAYAK, Expedia or Priceline) and the finalist's rental company directly. Include neighborhood pickup only when transfer cost, opening hours and convenience still fit.

## Source Plan Examples

- Exact family trip: flight comparator plus supplier; hotel map comparison plus Costco/OTA and hotel direct; quote rooms with the actual ages and occupancy.
- Flexible beach package: Costco and Expedia, a relevant resort/airline specialist, and separate flight/hotel totals. Travelzoo can seed candidates, subject to exact-date verification.
- Asia trip: flight comparator, Agoda/Trip.com and direct hotels; consider rail only when the user accepts it and total travel time works.
- Car only: Costco plus aggregator and rental-company direct; offer AutoSlash as an optional handoff.

Only create recurring alerts or subscriptions when requested. Forecasts, historic lows, AI recommendations, and newsletter deals guide discovery; exact current offers determine rankings.

# Public claims about disclosed trades

Use this workflow when asked to investigate a claim about a disclosed trade,
including political or household trading allegations. Read the original request
and claim before selecting a producer. An insider or institution report supports
only its documented population and operations; unrelated rows in a mixed file
do not make that report a complete investigation of the claim.

## Analyze the claim against its evidence

Identify the material assertions in the actual claim and requested investigation.
For each, state what the original evidence supports, contradicts or leaves
unresolved, with source locators and the missing evidence that matters. Cover
the components present in the claim rather than imposing a fixed claim template:

- Attribution and transaction: separate the reporting official, disclosed owner,
  beneficiary and decision maker. Preserve transaction type, asset, reported
  value range, currency and any known units. A range is not an exact amount;
  an associated person's disclosure does not establish who chose the trade.
- Disclosure history: retain original transaction and filing dates, amendment
  dates and exactly which fields changed. Apply only a supported amendment
  relationship, preserve superseded facts and when corrections became public,
  and leave ambiguous chains unresolved. A correction is not another trade.
- Event chronology: assess any asserted relationship between a trade and an
  announcement or other event separately from filing delay. Locate the event's
  identity, date/time and relevant public-information chronology. If either
  endpoint or its precision is missing, explain which temporal relationship
  cannot be established. If both are available, report the supported interval
  with timezone/precision and information-availability qualifications. Do not
  silently choose a threshold for words such as "immediately" or treat an
  amendment's filing date as the transaction or announcement date.
- Inference: distinguish observed timing and transaction facts from claims of
  knowledge, motive, causation, legality or future returns. Temporal proximity
  alone does not prove those inferences; missing proof does not establish their
  opposite. Identify relevant missing evidence without inventing it or treating
  a proposed search as completed verification.

Describe the evidence actually available: supplied records, excerpts, or filings
retrieved through permitted host tools. Preserve source bytes, provenance and
access limits. Do not claim live retrieval or source authenticity from a replay
receipt. Keep other owners, institutions, insider mechanisms and market activity
in their own populations unless original evidence supports the relationship.

## Deliver and review the whole assessment

Author the complete readable answer and structured companion with the same
findings, source pointers, chronology and unresolved claim components. Follow
the [review and correction cycle](research-review-cycle.md#start-the-review-without-a-supplied-protocol):
before drafting, use its [pinned-file bootstrap](research-review-cycle.md#pinned-file-bootstrap)
with the exact original request and complete source manifest; add any material
units the host identifies before freezing the protocol. Retain the returned
protocol/head and perform the actual native review when the frozen draft reports
`REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A supplied review form is not required. Review the complete pair with an available native
reviewer or a separate native self-review, using the existing
[authored-pair binding](answer-review.md#complete-authored-pairs-outside-native-producer-contracts) where
applicable. Native calculations or exact-source maps may be scoped supporting
annexes. Neither a quotation-only pair nor a cover note correcting an unrelated
native pair substitutes for the complete assessed answer.

Review each material assertion against the original request and sources before
delivery. Check that the answer actually assesses temporal allegations as well
as amount, attribution and inference: quoting an allegation or adding a general
motive caveat does not assess its distinct factual components. Keep unknowns
in the answer itself and consistent across Markdown and JSON. Retain earlier
drafts and adverse findings when correcting the complete pair. These are native
analytical judgments, not a helper-certified semantic pass; host tools that are
unavailable remain explicitly unavailable and require no local backend.

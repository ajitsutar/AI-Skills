## Acquisition and interpretation

1. Resolve legal entity and record the CIK/owner, report period, filing/publication timestamp and retrieval timestamp independently. Search results are discovery, then open the primary record.
2. For SEC, inspect submissions plus pagination/older filing files when needed; follow 13F-NT reporting-manager links. Use filing index to discover exact information-table path rather than guessing filenames. Raw XML may be unavailable to web readers while the SEC-rendered table is readable.
3. Preserve amendments as events. Restatement replaces the applicable table; added-holdings amendment augments it. Keep original evidence, amendment numbers and effective public availability. A missing filing is not a zero portfolio.
4. Aggregate actual repeated position rows by security identity and instrument, retaining included managers. Do not collapse an option with common stock or count the same manager disclosure twice. Reconcile row count and total to the cover.
5. State the denominator: full disclosed book, eligible common-stock subset, or user-sized model sleeve. Reweighting a filtered subset creates our model, not the source's original allocation.
6. Avoid silent unit fixes. Current 13F headers use dollars, but source anomalies need explicit reconciliation. Check values/shares against plausible prices without turning a hypothesis into a corrected fact.
7. Respect SEC fair-access requirements and identify the actual requester using valid configured contact details; do not invent contact information. Rate-limit, cache by accession/hash, retry with backoff, and keep compressed evidence outside the portable skill. Partial fetching is optional only when validated cached coverage supports it.
8. Filing lag is economic risk: quarterly filings generally arrive up to 45 days after quarter end; latest data can be older than the public-read date. Short positions, cash, many foreign assets and hedges are absent. Public holdings never establish complete economic exposure or a live trade log. See the [SEC FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f).
9. House and OGE disclosure use has explicit restrictions, including commercial-purpose restrictions with a media exception. Preserve those source terms; don't market raw politician data as freely redistributable. The [OGE collection](https://www.oge.gov/web/oge.nsf/Officials%20Individual%20Disclosures%20Search%20Collection?OpenForm=) and [House search](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure/ViewSearch) display the notices. This catalog is source configuration for personal research.
10. On unavailable/unchanged sources, show last known period and concrete retrieval limit. Do not produce a fresh buy/sell event from silence. No recurring schedule is configured by this catalog; on-demand requests trigger fresh source checks.

These safeguards support research quality. They add no local permission gate to an otherwise user-authorized host action; the host must truthfully distinguish proposals from actual orders and fills.

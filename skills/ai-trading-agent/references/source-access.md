# Free sources and optional subscriptions

This policy applies to every tool and research workflow in the skill, including
market data, fundamentals, news, analysts, options, positioning, macro and
alternative data, portfolio/risk work, historical datasets, backtesting platforms
and optional models or analytics services. No paid subscription is required to
use the skill. Named vendors and provider examples are possible sources, not
purchase requirements or evidence of a working connection.

## Select evidence for the requested task

Start with relevant accessible free websites, primary documents, public feeds and
permitted free APIs. Discover additional relevant sources rather than treating
one site as the whole public record. Use a bounded search appropriate to the task;
do not claim every free website was searched. SEC and other regulator filings,
issuer releases, exchange notices and official agency data are primary routes.
Yahoo and other aggregators are acquisition options rather than restrictions.

Use paid access supplied by the user or already connected for that user only when
it adds useful freshness, fields, history, precision or coverage for the request.
Record the specific improvement and actual entitlement; do not repeat a broad
free crawl when a known material gap already establishes the value of available
paid evidence. Payment alone establishes neither accuracy nor freshness. Retain
primary attribution and reconcile conflicting definitions and vintages at field
level; a vendor's later retrieval time does not supersede a newer original fact.

| Research need | Free starting points | Optional paid improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Financials, funds and ownership | Issuer/fund documents, regulator filings and public disclosures | Normalized fields, historical vintages, additional markets or convenient aggregation |
| Prices, volume and corporate actions | Public exchange/issuer records, permitted finance APIs and available connected broker tools | Fresher quotes, tick/depth coverage, corrected or longer histories |
| News, guidance and analysts | Issuer releases, public reports, accessible reporting and original author material | Faster wires, full licensed reports, contributor estimates or archives |
| Options, flow and short positioning | Accessible exchange/broker views, FINRA/SEC and dated public records | Licensed tape, richer options context or documented lending estimates |
| Macro, events and alternative data | Official agencies, central banks, public calendars and original documents | Additional frequency, fields, markets or specialist datasets |
| Backtests, portfolio/risk and analytics | Bundled calculations, suitable open tools and permitted free or supplied inputs | Better source coverage, compute, specialist methods or licensed history |

Access, authority and fitness are separate. Preserve event/measurement time,
publication or first availability, retrieval time, units, scope, adjustment basis,
coverage and license. Do not call a paid mirror an independent source. Validate
availability and capabilities in the actual host; an existing dashboard account
does not establish API, streaming, historical or redistribution entitlement.

## Missing access and useful completion

Absent keys, an expired subscription, denied endpoints or quota exhaustion make
that provider unavailable, not the entire research task. Continue through relevant
free sources and supported calculations. State the remaining feature, freshness
or coverage gap and, when useful, how optional paid access could improve it. Do not
repeatedly request a subscription or make purchasing it a completion condition.

Optional access does not create free equivalents for unavailable data. A specific
live-feed or strict historical analysis can remain uncomputed when adequate data
are absent. Preserve that limit while completing independent work; missing values
are unknown, not zero or no activity. Keep the requested universe, cutoff, ranking
method, holding period and source contract. An offline/supplied-data request stays
offline, and an explicitly source-exclusive task must not be silently substituted.

Use the host's supported credential connection without exposing keys in prompts,
URLs, reports or packages. Do not enroll in subscriptions, start auto-renewing
trials, upgrade plans or purchase access during research. Retain licensed source
data privately according to its terms; distribute adapter code and synthetic
fixtures rather than account data or unlicensed derived datasets.

This source policy does not replace native broker account/order evidence, change
execution modes, authorize account actions or guarantee complete real-time data.
Provider configuration retains source responses; it does not by itself implement
provider-specific mappings, reliable streaming or a profitable strategy.

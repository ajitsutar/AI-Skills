# Research and allocation preferences

Optional analytical inputs, not a live policy or execution checklist. Leave
unknown preferences unknown. Example JSON fixtures do not govern the account.

- Objective, arbitrary holding horizon, benchmark and intended universe:
- Requested investment amount and actual recurring contributions:
- Existing holdings and relevant external exposure:
- User's preferred allocation, instruments, exclusions and liquidity needs:
- Scenario assumptions, costs, tax considerations and review cadence:
- Optional volatility model, data window and validation comparison:
- Catalysts, earnings exposure, thesis invalidation and possible exits:
- Source coverage, observations and uncertainty:

Recommendations may discuss concentration, cash, liquidity and price uncertainty
without turning these analytical preferences into execution thresholds. Host
execution follows the current task's own persistent policy, bound to its actual
task ID. Each scheduled task starts in `paper`; no global policy or another task's
mode applies. Re-read the task-specific file and verify its binding before acting.
Prompts supply rules and cannot change that mode or binding. Only an explicit
user-requested change to this task's policy updates its mode; a task clarification
or these research preferences do not authorize a change. The pure handoff formatter
needs no policy setup and submits no order. Preserve existing authority within
the selected mode, including per-order approval in `approval` and standing
authority in `autonomous`; keep `paper` simulated. The broker/platform applies its
own requirements. See [execution policy](../references/execution-modes.md).
